## Background

In my second year I took Software Engineering, where we practiced **Scrum**, architecture, and professional documentation. Inspired by delivery apps in Venezuela, we designed **UcabGo**: students can order from campus vendors and other students handle delivery.

## Customer experience

Users sign in, browse venues and products, and place orders.
