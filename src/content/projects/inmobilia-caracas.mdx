## Motivation

My mother works as a real estate advisor in Caracas and juggles many properties and clients. She needed a place to manage listings and present them professionally to buyers. I needed real-world frontend practice—so we built **Inmobilia Caracas** together.

It was my first serious **UI/UX** work, my first time keeping a tight feedback loop with a non-technical client, and my first production deployment.
